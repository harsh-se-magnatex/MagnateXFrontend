## The short version

| | Social media agency | AI social media software |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly cost | Often $500–2,000 | About $15–85 |
| Speed | Days per round of revisions | Posts planned a month ahead, created in minutes |
| Consistency | Depends on their workload | Runs every week, including your busiest |
| Brand knowledge | Learned over months, can change with staff | Learned at setup from your website and designs |
| Control | You approve their drafts | You approve every post, or delegate review |
| Strategy and ads | Strong — senior people, campaign thinking | Content and publishing; strategy is lighter |

## What you get from an agency

A good agency brings people: a strategist, a designer, a copywriter, sometimes a photographer and an ads specialist. That is real value for complex launches, paid campaigns and brands that need big creative ideas.

The trade-offs for a growing business:

- **Cost.** A retainer priced like a part-time hire, for a team that works your account part time.
- **Their calendar.** Your posts get planned around their other clients — revisions take days, and festival posts can slip.
- **Turnover.** When your account manager changes, so can your voice.

## What you get from AI social media software

AI software does the repetitive production: deciding what to post, writing, designing, scheduling and reading the analytics. With SocioGenie's AI Manager, that means a full monthly plan, each post generated two days before it goes live, review before publishing, and posting at your best hour.

The trade-offs:

- **Context is on you, once.** It needs your website, product photos and brand look at setup.
- **Strategy is lighter.** It plans content well; it won't run a multi-channel brand campaign or your paid ads.
- **Trust is earned.** Review closely in the first month, then loosen.

## When an agency is the right choice

- You're launching a new brand and need positioning, naming or a big creative campaign.
- Paid ads are your main channel and you need someone managing spend daily.
- You need on-site photo or video shoots every month.

## When AI is the right choice

- You already have products and things to say, but not the time to post consistently.
- Your feed goes quiet when the week gets busy.
- You want to see and approve every post without producing them.
- Your budget is closer to a tool subscription than a retainer.

## The hybrid many businesses use

Plenty of businesses do both: AI handles the steady, weekly posting, and an agency or freelancer is hired for the occasional big campaign or photo shoot. That keeps the feed active every week without paying a full retainer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AI cheaper than a social media agency?

Yes, by a wide margin. AI social media software typically costs $15–85 a month; agency retainers commonly start around $500. The difference is what you give up — mainly senior strategy and paid-ads management.

### Can AI replace a social media agency completely?

For consistent organic posting, often yes. For brand strategy, big creative campaigns and paid ads, an agency or specialist still adds value. Many businesses use AI for the weekly work and hire help for the occasional campaign.

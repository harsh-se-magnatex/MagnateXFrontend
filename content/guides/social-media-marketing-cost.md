## The four ways to pay for social media

| Approach | Typical monthly cost | Your time each week | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do it yourself | $0 + your time | 5–10 hours | Very early businesses with time to spare |
| Freelancer | Varies widely, often per post | 1–2 hours to brief and approve | Occasional design or content help |
| Agency | Often $500–2,000 | 1–2 hours, on their schedule | Big campaigns, paid ads, launches |
| AI social media software | About $15–85 | From under an hour | Consistent posting without a team |

## Doing it yourself

**Cost:** free tools, plus your time.

The real price is hours: planning, photographing, writing, designing, posting and replying — typically five to ten hours a week for one or two platforms. It works while you have the time. It breaks in the busy weeks, which is when the feed goes quiet.

## Hiring a freelancer

**Cost:** varies widely by skill and market — some charge per post, others a monthly fee.

A good freelancer brings design skill and saves you production time. The risks are availability and consistency: when they're busy or away, your posting stops.

## Hiring an agency

**Cost:** retainers commonly run from about $500 to $2,000 a month for one brand; in India, roughly ₹15,000–40,000 a month.

You get a team and a strategy. You also get their calendar: revisions take days, and your posts are planned around their other clients. Best value when you need big campaigns, paid ads or regular shoots.

## Using AI social media software

**Cost:** roughly $15–85 a month.

AI software handles the production — planning, writing, design, scheduling and analytics. You provide context once and approve the output. SocioGenie's pricing, for example:

| Plan | Monthly price | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Studio | $14.99 | Seven AI creation tools and 100 credits; you create and schedule |
| Prime AI | $49.99 | AI Manager runs your calendar on one platform |
| Elite AI | $69.99 | AI Manager on two platforms |
| Legacy AI | $84.99 | AI Manager on all three platforms |

Plans are billed in US dollars. In India, Studio is roughly ₹1,250 and Prime AI roughly ₹4,200 a month, depending on the exchange rate. Credit packs for extra on-demand content start at $6.99.

## Hidden costs to watch for

- **Your time.** The cheapest option on paper is often the most expensive in hours.
- **Ad spend.** Paid promotion is separate from management fees in every model.
- **Photography.** Good product photos are the raw material for every approach.
- **Inconsistency.** Weeks with no posts cost reach and trust — hard to price, easy to feel.

## Which option is right for you?

- **You have time and enjoy it:** do it yourself, with free tools.
- **You need occasional creative help:** a freelancer.
- **You need campaigns, ads and strategy:** an agency.
- **You need consistent, on-brand posting without hiring:** AI social media software.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much should a small business spend on social media marketing?

It depends on whether you're paying with money or time. Many growing businesses start with AI software in the $15–85 range for steady posting, and add ad spend or agency help for specific campaigns once they know what works.

### How much does a social media manager cost per month?

A freelance or agency social media manager commonly costs several hundred to a few thousand dollars a month, depending on scope and market. AI social media managers cost a fraction of that, starting around $50 a month for a fully managed calendar.

### Is social media marketing worth the cost?

For most businesses that sell to consumers, yes — an active profile builds trust with every customer who looks you up, and consistent posting compounds over months. The key is choosing an approach you can sustain.

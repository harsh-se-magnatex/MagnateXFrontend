## 5. Security Measures

We maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- **Encryption** — TLS 1.2 or higher in transit; encryption at rest for stored data, assets, and backups.
- **Access control** — role-based, least-privilege access; hashed passwords; separate credentials per environment; access reviews.
- **Secret management** — API keys and OAuth tokens held in managed secret storage, never in application code or logs.
- **Logging** — audit logging of administrative and review actions.
- **Segregation** — customer data logically segregated by account.
- **Resilience** — managed cloud infrastructure with automated backups and documented restoration.
- **Vendor management** — security review before engaging a sub-processor.
- **Personnel** — confidentiality obligations, least-privilege access, and offboarding revocation.

We may update these measures provided the overall level of security is not reduced.

We will make available to you the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28, including our Information Security Statement, our sub-processor list, and any third-party attestations our providers publish.

You bear your own costs; we may charge reasonable costs for time spent beyond one business day.

## 9. Deletion and Return

On termination or expiry of the Services, we will delete personal data processed on your behalf within **30 days** of confirmed account deletion, except:

- encrypted backups, which are rotated out within **90 days**;
- data we are required to retain by law, including billing and tax records retained for up to **8 years**;
- data subject to a legal hold, retained until the matter is resolved.

You may export your data before deletion using the in-product export tooling.

**Content already published to your social-media accounts is outside our control and is not deleted by this process.** You must delete it from the platform yourself.

## 10. Personal-Data Breach

We will notify you **without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours**, of becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting personal data processed on your behalf. The notification will describe, to the extent known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected, likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

We will cooperate with you and take reasonable steps to mitigate the effects and minimise the damage.

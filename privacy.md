## 3. What We Collect

### 3.1 Information you provide

**Account data** — name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed), profile picture, country, time zone, language.

**Business and brand data** — business name, business email, business contact number, industry, website URL, location, brand slogan, brand description and voice, default hashtags, brand colours, visual-style selection, logo files, product images, product descriptions, and any other asset uploaded to your Media Library.

**Uploaded catalogues** — where you upload a catalogue PDF during onboarding, we extract text and images from it to build your Brand DNA. Do not upload catalogues containing personal data, pricing you consider confidential, or third-party material you are not licensed to use.

**Questionnaire answers** — your responses about products, customers, objections, themes, preferred phrasing, seasonal campaigns, and product lines to feature.

**Content data** — prompts, reference text, uploaded reference images, generated captions and creatives, drafts, approvals, rejections, regeneration requests, scheduled posts, and published posts.

**Communications** — support emails, chat-assistant transcripts, and feedback.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

**Device and technical data** — IP address, device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution, language, referring URL, error and crash logs.

**Usage data** — features accessed, generation events, credits consumed, approval actions, posts scheduled and published, session activity.

**Cookies and similar technologies** — see our [Cookie Policy](https://www.sociogenie.ai/legal/cookie).

### 3.3 Information from third parties

**Social-media platforms.** When you connect Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn via OAuth, we receive access tokens, account and page/organisation identifiers, basic profile data, granted permission scopes, and — where you authorise it — post-level insights such as reach, impressions, and engagement. We use tokens only to perform actions you have authorised. **We never receive or store your social-media password.**

**Website and brand extraction.** If you provide a website URL during onboarding, we fetch publicly available pages through third-party extraction services (Firecrawl and OpenBrand) to pull business name, industry, description, logos, colours, imagery, and contact details into your Brand DNA.

**Payment processor.** Purchases are handled by **Dodo Payments Inc.**, our Merchant of Record. Dodo collects your billing name, address, country, tax identifiers, and payment-instrument details directly. We receive only a limited transaction record — plan, amount, currency, last four digits, status, invoice ID. **We never see or store your full card number, CVV, or bank credentials.**

## 5. AI Processing

SocioGenie sends prompts, brand context, and — where relevant — your uploaded images and assets to enterprise AI providers acting as our processors:

- **Anthropic, PBC** (United States) — text generation, content strategy, reasoning.
- **Google LLC — Vertex AI / Gemini API** (United States) — multimodal generation across text, image, and video.
- **OpenAI, L.L.C.** (United States) — text and image processing for specific features.

**We do not use your personal data, brand assets, or generated content to train our own AI models.** Our AI providers are contractually prevented from training their general-purpose models on your data under the configurations we use, and we enable API training opt-outs and zero-retention settings where the provider makes them available.

**Automated decision-making.** The AI Engine decides what to post, when, and how to present it. In Manual Review mode nothing publishes without your approval. In Auto Approve mode, content is published on schedule following the limited human review described in Section 6. These decisions concern your marketing content, not you as an individual, and do not produce legal or similarly significant effects on any person within the meaning of GDPR Article 22. You can disable automation at any time.

For what generated content can and cannot be relied on, see our [AI Disclosure](https://www.sociogenie.ai/legal/ai-disclosure).

## 6. Human Review

Where your plan includes human-reviewed content, review is carried out by **employees of MAGNATEX LLP working in India**. We do not use an external agency, contractor pool, or crowd workforce for review.

Reviewers see only the generated post — caption and creative. They do not access your social-media inbox, message threads, follower or audience data, contact lists, billing information, or account credentials. All reviewers are bound by written confidentiality obligations and work under least-privilege access controls with review actions logged.

Review covers brand consistency, caption quality, creative presentation, and readiness to post. It is not a fact-check, legal review, or rights clearance.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

- **Access** — obtain a copy of your personal data.
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure** — delete personal data, subject to legal retention.
- **Restriction** — limit how we process your data.
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to direct marketing at any time.
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing.
- **Complain** — to your supervisory authority: the Data Protection Board of India, your EU or UK supervisory authority, or the California Privacy Protection Agency.

**California residents** additionally have the right to know, delete, correct, and limit use of sensitive personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

**Indian Data Principals** may nominate another individual to exercise their rights in the event of death or incapacity, and may contact our Grievance Officer (Section 13).

To exercise a right, email <founder@magnatex.co> from your Account email address. We respond within **30 days**, or sooner where the law requires. We may need to verify your identity first.

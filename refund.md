## 1. General Position

SocioGenie is a digital, AI-powered software service delivered immediately on purchase. Subscription fees and Credit purchases are prepaid and, as a general rule, **non-refundable**.

We do not refund for:

- dissatisfaction with the quality, tone, style, or accuracy of AI-generated content;

- change of mind or buyer's remorse;
- unused portions of a billing cycle;
- decisions made by Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or any other platform.

We do issue refunds in the limited circumstances in Sections 4 and 5, and we will always honour rights that mandatory law gives you.

All purchases are processed by **Dodo Payments Inc.**, acting as **Merchant of Record**. All charges are billed and settled in **United States Dollars (USD)**.

The amount credited in your local currency may differ from the amount charged due to exchange-rate movement, and foreign-transaction or conversion fees charged by your bank are not reimbursable by us.

## 4. Consumers in the EU and UK: Right of Withdrawal

If you are a consumer resident in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract within **14 days** of entering into it, without giving a reason.

**How this interacts with immediate access.** SocioGenie is supplied immediately on purchase. At checkout you expressly request that supply begin during the 14-day withdrawal period and acknowledge that:

- for **digital content supplied in full** (for example, a Credit pack made available in your Account immediately), you **lose the right of withdrawal** once supply has begun with your consent;
- for the **ongoing subscription service**, you may withdraw within 14 days, and we will refund the price paid **less a proportionate amount** for the service already supplied up to the moment you tell us.

To withdraw, email <founder@magnatex.co> from your Account email within 14 days of purchase, stating that you wish to withdraw. We will process the refund via Dodo within 14 days of receiving your notice, to the original payment method.

**This section takes precedence over Sections 1, 5 and 6 for EU and UK consumers.**

## 5. Other Situations Where We Refund

We will consider a refund, at our discretion, in the following cases.

### 5.1 Duplicate or accidental charges

If you were charged more than once for the same subscription period or the same Credit pack due to a billing error, we refund the duplicate in full on verification. Contact us at any time.

### 5.2 Verified technical failure

If you paid for the Services but could not use them for a material period because of a reproducible defect or outage **attributable solely to SocioGenie** — not to a third-party platform, your network, your browser, or a disconnected social account — and our support team cannot resolve it in a reasonable time, we may issue a full or partial refund, extend your subscription, or re-credit affected Credits.

### 5.3 Credits deducted in error

Where Credits are consumed because of a verified failure on our side — a generation that never completed, a duplicate deduction, a scheduled post that failed to publish for reasons within our control — we **re-credit the affected Credits to your Account**. We do not convert Credits back to cash.

### 5.4 Unauthorised transactions

If a charge was made on your Account without your authorisation, contact <founder@magnatex.co> **within 7 days** of the charge. Following investigation, which may require identity verification and involve Dodo Payments, we refund verified unauthorised charges.

### 5.5 Material adverse change to your plan

If we materially reduce the core functionality of your plan during a paid cycle, you may cancel and receive a pro-rated refund of the unused portion of that cycle.

### 5.6 Statutory rights

Where mandatory consumer-protection law in your country of residence grants a refund right that cannot be excluded by contract, that right applies regardless of anything in this Policy.

## 6. Credits

**Plan Credits** are included with your subscription, reset at the start of each billing cycle, do not roll over, and are not refundable. They expire when your subscription expires, is cancelled, or is suspended.

**Top-Up Credits** are valid for **30 days from the date of purchase**, are held in a separate pool from Plan Credits, and require an active subscription to be used. Expired Top-Up Credits are not restored, extended, or refunded.

Credits have no cash value and are not transferable between Accounts.

Section 5.3 and Section 4 are the only exceptions to the above.

## 7. Subscriptions

### 7.1 Cancellation

You may cancel at any time from account settings or by emailing <support@magnatex.co>. Cancellation stops future renewals. It does not refund past charges. You keep access and your remaining Plan Credits until the end of the current paid cycle.

### 7.2 Upgrades and downgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately and are billed pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle; no partial refund is issued for unused days at the higher tier.

### 7.3 Renewal reminders

Where required by law in your market, we send advance notice before an automatic renewal. Failure to cancel before a renewal is not, by itself, grounds for a refund except where local law provides otherwise.

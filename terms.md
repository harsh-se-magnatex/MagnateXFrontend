## 11. Approval Modes and Automated Publishing

**This section describes the most significant risk in using the Services. Read it before enabling Auto Approve.**

**Manual Review** — no content is published until you review and approve it. This is the safer configuration and we recommend it for regulated sectors and for any business where factual accuracy in a post carries commercial or legal consequence.

**Auto Approve** — content is reviewed by SocioGenie employees against the limited criteria in Section 11.1 and then published to your Connected Accounts on schedule, **without you seeing it first**. By enabling Auto Approve you acknowledge and accept that:

- content will be published under your brand's name to your audience, and will be indistinguishable to that audience from content you wrote;
- you are electing in advance to publish material you have not individually read;
- errors, inaccuracies, unsuitable timing, or content that is inappropriate for a particular market may be published before you or we become aware of it;
- automated publishing continues during holidays, news events, and crises unless you pause it, and it is your responsibility to pause it;
- **you remain the publisher of record** for all purposes, including platform rules, advertising law, consumer-protection law, and the disclosure obligations described in Section 19.

You may switch to Manual Review, pause the AI Engine, or disconnect any Connected Account at any time.

### 11.1 Scope of human review

Human review is performed by **employees of SocioGenie**, bound by confidentiality obligations. Reviewers see the generated post — caption and creative — and do not access your social inbox, message threads, audience data, or account credentials.

Review is limited to **brand consistency, caption quality, creative presentation, and technical readiness to post**. It is expressly **not** a fact-check, a legal or regulatory review, an advertising-compliance review, a trademark or copyright clearance, or a verification that a product depiction matches your actual product. A post passing review carries no representation by us that it is accurate, lawful, or compliant.
